# Arcade typography A

Exo 2: Latin letters and numbers, variable upright and italic. M PLUS 1p: Japanese, Medium / Bold / ExtraBold. Medium covers body text; Bold and ExtraBold cover labels and headings. Only prominent actions and numerical displays are italic; explanatory text remains upright.

Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/exo2 and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mplus1p . Downloaded 2026-11-07. Original font data losslessly packaged as WOFF2 using fontTools. Licenses retained as exo2-OFL.txt and mplus1p-OFL.txt. Fonts are bundled locally for offline/PWA and iOS use. Existing generated title and image labels remain intact.
